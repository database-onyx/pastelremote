The Spirit of Internal Innovation

Intrapreneurship is the act of engaging in entrepreneurship within an established organization. It empowers employees to take initiative and develop new ideas, products, processes, or business lines using the company’s resources without bearing personal financial risk.

Key Traits of Intrapreneurs

Think like owners, not just employees
Spot opportunities inside the organization
Push innovation and challenge the status quo
Build and test ideas using company team and budget

Intrapreneurship vs Entrepreneurship

Category Intrapreneur Entrepreneur
Risk Company takes financial risk Personal financial risk
Resources Uses company’s money and team Raises own funding
Ownership Company owns the IP/result Owns the business
Goal Grow/change inside existing firm Build new independent venture

Real-World Examples

Gmail & Google Maps: Started as "20% time" side projects by Google employees.
Sony PlayStation: Developed by a Sony engineer who pushed the idea internally against initial resistance.

Why Organizations Choose It

Faster innovation without starting from scratch
Retains top talent seeking high-impact roles
Opens new, untapped revenue streams

Encouraging Intrapreneurship

Schools and Colleges

Innovative Learning Models: Develop new teaching methods or curricula to improve student engagement.

EdTech Integration: Implement technology solutions to enhance learning experiences.

Student Entrepreneurship: Encourage students to develop entrepreneurial skills and ideas.

Hospitals

Patient Experience: Improve patient satisfaction and outcomes through innovative services.

Healthcare Technology: Implement digital health solutions like telemedicine or patient portals.

Operational Efficiency: Streamline processes and reduce costs through innovative management practices.
